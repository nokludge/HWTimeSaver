# Privacy Policy for "Canvas Capture"

**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the **Canvas Capture** Chrome Extension handles your data.

### 1. 100% Local Processing (No Data Collection)
**Canvas Capture** operates entirely within your local browser sandbox. 
* We **do not** collect, store, log, or track any personal data, browsing history, or analytical metrics.
* We **do not** use any external servers, tracking cookies, or analytics scripts.

### 2. No Data Transmission
All screenshot capturing and cropping processes happen locally on your machine. The extension does not transmit images or data over the internet. Once cropped, the image is placed directly onto your system's clipboard.

### 3. Permissions Used
To function correctly, the extension requires the following Chrome permissions:
* **`activeTab`:** Used exclusively to capture the visible area of the webpage you are currently looking at when you click the capture button.
* **`scripting`:** Used to detect the layout coordinates and dimensions of the `<canvas id="gameCanvas">` element on the active page to ensure pixel-perfect cropping.
* **`clipboardWrite`:** Used solely to save the finished cropped screenshot into your device's clipboard so you can paste it anywhere.

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
